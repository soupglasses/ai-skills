# 14 Writing respects the reader's time

Decided 2026-11-14.

**Decision.** Everything in this repository is dense and to the point, and keeps only what
matters to its reader. Every word is chosen. Length follows from the reader and the
purpose, never from a line cap.

**Why.** These files are loaded into an agent's context, and a reader's time is limited. A
line cap is a measure standing in for the goal, and an agent meets the measure instead.

**Carried by.** `skills/write-instructions`, `skills/commit-and-pr`, `instructions/CLAUDE.md`
(Writing).
